The crab pots come up for the last time in early October, and the creek
doesn't empty out. It changes hands. As the water cools the striped bass,
rockfish to everyone on this shore, move down the rivers toward the Bay and
feed hard before winter, and for the whole run the water in front of
the cottage is part of that traffic.

We didn't plan the cottage around it. The dock was for crabbing and coffee.
But leave a rod on the porch rail in October and somebody picks it up at
dusk, and a nineteen-inch rockfish pulled onto your own dock, with the
kitchen lit up behind you, is a different evening than the same fish
anywhere else.

## Two ways to fish it

From the dock, it's simple: you're on a tidal creek off the Potomac, casting
into the same water the <a href={withBase("/journal/crabbers-morning-on-hull-creek")}>crab
pots sat in all summer</a>, an hour before dinner or an hour after coffee.
Nothing about it requires a boat.

For the real thing, drive fifteen minutes to <a href={withBase("/journal/complete-guide-to-reedville-virginia")}>Reedville</a>,
where the charter fleet ties up. The captains run the lower Potomac and the
Bay to the end of the season, and they know where the fish went yesterday.
We know a few of them. Tell us before your stay and we'll put you in touch.

## The rules, in one paragraph

The numbers are the same everywhere on this water: a keeper is between
19 and 24 inches, and the limit is one fish per person per day. Hull Creek
is fed by the Potomac, so we go by Virginia's Potomac tributary
season here, already open since August 21 and running through December 31. The
Bay-side season, for context, opens later: October 4. On a charter the
captain carries the rules for you.
On the license: you don't need to buy one to fish from our dock. Virginia
exempts anyone fishing from property they own or rent, and during your stay
that's you. What you do need is the free FIP registration, which covers
anyone sixteen or over fishing without a license. It takes a minute <a
href="https://www.mrc.virginia.gov/fip/">online</a> and costs nothing. On a
licensed Reedville charter you need neither, because the boat covers you.
And if you'd rather not think about any of it, a ten-day saltwater license
runs ten dollars.

## What the rest of the day looks like

A fall fishing day here doesn't end at the water. The hot tub on the patio
beside the porch is open all year, and the cedar sauna has a window on the
creek. Cook the fish in the kitchen. Then the fire pit, then the porch, then
whatever the tide does overnight.
