# Checkout thank-you + review ask

Proactive, not reactive: send the morning of checkout (or right after).
Review-text sentiment feeds Airbnb ranking, so the nudge to *name* an
amenity matters. Adapt the middle sentence to something specific from
their stay when known (weather, kids, what they asked about).

---

## Q&A: checkout-day message

**Question patterns** (when to use — not guest-asked):
- guest checks out today
- stay just ended, no message thread open
- guest said goodbye / thanks in the thread

**Canonical answer:**

> Thanks so much for staying with us. We hope the creek treated you well.
> If you have a minute, a review goes a long way for a small place like
> ours, and if the sauna or the sunset off the dock made your stay,
> mentioning it helps other travelers find what they're looking for.
> Safe travels home. — Will
