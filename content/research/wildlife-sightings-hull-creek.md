# Wildlife sightings at Captain's Cottage — owner record

Firsthand sightings Will has recorded over the years at the cottage on Hull Creek. Each is treated as an owner-confirmed observation; researcher must still verify any seasonality, biology, or behavior claims a writer makes from them, but the sighting list itself is owner-firsthand.

## Rare / once-in-years moments
- **Seahorse** — came up in the crab pot, once.
- **Sea turtle** — washed into the creek, once.
- **Sea otter** — spotted from shore in winter, once.
- **Fox** — seen in the cornfield, once.
- **Swans** — pair drifted past, once (also captured in the ospreys post).
- **Kingfisher** — seen on the water once (already in the ospreys species list).

## Seasonal / migratory
- **Cownose rays** — migrate past in early May.

## Resident / common
- **Rabbits**, **squirrels** — around the house regularly.
- **Hummingbirds** — at the property.
- **Osprey**, **bald eagle**, **great blue heron**, **egret** — already the working dock list (see `mornings-with-the-ospreys-hull-creek.md`).

## Notes for the Writer

- The "once-in-years" moments are the gift of a long-residency post — a piece that hits the abundance-never-apology rule because the whole point is *what shows up if you stay long enough*. Voice: a list-in-prose of small wonders.
- Cownose ray migration is the only reliably seasonal item — anchors an "early May on the creek" angle.
- Hummingbirds and rabbits/squirrels are background — useful texture in other posts; weak as a standalone subject.
- DO NOT fabricate dates or counts. If a writer needs specificity (the year of the seahorse, the size of the sea turtle), mark it as `{/* TODO: Will to confirm */}` instead of inventing.
