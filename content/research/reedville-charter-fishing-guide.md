### Licence
- Code of Virginia § 28.2-302.5 exempts "A person fishing from a licensed headboat, charterboat, or pier licensed pursuant to § 28.2-302 or 28.2-302.8" (quoted in `fall-rockfish-northern-neck.md`; https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title28.2/chapter3/section28.2-302.5/).
- VMRC FIP page (https://www.mrc.virginia.gov/fip/, fetched 2026-10-03): "paying customers on a licensed charter or head boat also DO NOT have to register." So the safe statement is: paying passengers on a licensed charter don't need a license or FIP registration; confirm the boat is licensed when booking.
