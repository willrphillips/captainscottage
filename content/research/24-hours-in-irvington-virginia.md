### Irvington Town Character

- Irvington sits where Carter's Creek meets the Rappahannock River, approximately 12 miles north of the Chesapeake Bay. Originally called Carter's Creek (named for John Carter Sr., son of Robert "King" Carter), it was later renamed for Captain Levin Irvington.
  Source: [The Guide to Living in Irvington, Virginia — Rappahannock River Company](https://www.rappahannock.co/destinations/virginia/irvington); [Virginia's River Realm — Irvington](https://www.virginiasriverrealm.com/irvington-virginia/)

- Irvington is home to two nationally recognized inns: The Tides Inn (built 1947, the area's only 4-star property) and the Hope & Glory Inn (named "The South's Best Inn" by Southern Living Magazine multiple times). This density of recognized hospitality in a town this small is the heart of the Reedville contrast — Reedville has character; Irvington has polish.
  Source: [Virginia's River Realm — Irvington](https://www.virginiasriverrealm.com/irvington-virginia/); [Hope & Glory Inn](https://www.hopeandglory.com/)

- Irvington was historically a steamboat port — one of the Chesapeake's main passenger and cargo stops in the late 19th/early 20th century. That history is the town's origin story and the Steamboat Era Museum's reason for existing.
  Source: [Virginia's River Realm — Irvington](https://www.virginiasriverrealm.com/irvington-virginia/)

### Hope & Glory Inn

- 65 Tavern Road, Irvington; nationally recognized boutique inn in a former 1890 boarding school building. Named "Top 10 in the US" seven times (UNVERIFIED — source attribution unclear from web results; Travel + Leisure and Southern Living both referenced). "The South's Best Inn" — Southern Living.
  Source: [hopeandglory.com](https://www.hopeandglory.com/); [TripAdvisor — Hope & Glory Inn](https://www.tripadvisor.com/Hotel_Review-g57848-d82866-Reviews-Hope_And_Glory_Inn-Irvington_Virginia.html)

  Source: [hopeandglory.com](https://www.hopeandglory.com/)

### Historic Christ Church

- Address: 420 Christ Church Road, Weems, VA 22576 (approximately 5 miles from Irvington — not walking distance, a short drive).
  Source: [christchurch1735.org/visit](https://www.christchurch1735.org/visit)

- A National Historic Landmark. Built 1732–1735, commissioned by Robert "King" Carter, considered the finest surviving colonial church in the American South. Georgian architecture, remarkably intact.
  Source: [Wikipedia — Christ Church (Lancaster County, Virginia)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Christ_Church_(Lancaster_County,_Virginia)); [christchurch1735.org](https://www.christchurch1735.org/)

- May–October hours: Mon, Thu, Fri, Sat 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM; Sun 1:00 PM – 4:00 PM; Tue–Wed by appointment. Last tour 3:30 PM. Grounds open year-round.
  Source: [christchurch1735.org/visit/directions-hours](https://christchurch1735.org/visit/directions-hours)

- Admission: Adults $10; ages 18 and under and active/retired military free; groups (10+) $8/adult.
  Source: [christchurch1735.org/visit/directions-hours](https://christchurch1735.org/visit/directions-hours)

- Robert "King" Carter connection: Carter was one of the most powerful (and wealthy) men in colonial Virginia; the church is his direct commission and his tomb is on-site. This is the post's "historical gravitas" anchor — a counterpoint to the wine-and-oysters modern Irvington.
  Source: [christchurch1735.org/about/the-church](https://www.christchurch1735.org/about/the-church)

### Steamboat Era Museum

- Address: 156 King Carter Drive, Irvington, VA 22480. Phone: (804) 438-6888. On the main commercial strip, walkable from The Tides Inn and The Local.
  Source: [steamboateramuseum.org](https://steamboateramuseum.org/)

- Hours (2026 season, opens May 1):
  - Spring/Fall: Fri, Sat, Mon 10 AM–4 PM; Sun 1 PM–4 PM
  - Summer: Thu, Fri, Sat, Mon 10 AM–4 PM; Sun 1 PM–4 PM
  Source: [steamboateramuseum.org/visit](https://steamboateramuseum.org/visit/)

- Admission: Adults $7.50; Youth (12–17) $3.50; Active military and children under 12 free; Groups (10+) $6.00/person.
  Source: [steamboateramuseum.org/visit](https://steamboateramuseum.org/visit/)

- The museum features the original 1894 Potomac Pilothouse (interactive — visitors can operate controls), exhibits on the Chesapeake steamboat era including the story of African-American steamboat owner Hansford Bayton. Compact museum, 1–1.5 hours is sufficient. Appropriate for a slot in a 24-hour itinerary without consuming the whole day.
  Source: [steamboateramuseum.org/visit](https://steamboateramuseum.org/visit/)

### Carter's Creek Waterfront

- Carter's Creek connects to the Rappahannock River. The Tides Inn sits directly on it; the waterfront is accessible by boat (the inn has a marina with 24 transient slips) and on foot along the resort property.
  Source: [marinas.com — Tides Inn Marina](https://marinas.com/view/marina/pwc6ye_Tides_Inn_Marina_Irvington_VA_United_States); [tidesinn.com](https://tidesinn.com/)
