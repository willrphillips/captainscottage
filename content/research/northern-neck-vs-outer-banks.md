## Key facts

### From local sources (authoritative)
- Northern Neck drive from DC: 165 minutes, no Bay Bridge, no tunnel. Route: I-95 S → US-17 S → VA-360 W (or US-301 S via Harry W. Nice Bridge). Source: `src/lib/site.ts` DRIVE_TIMES + `src/content/blog/northern-neck-long-weekend-from-dc.mdx` (Harry W. Nice Bridge named, no tunnel/Bay Bridge required)
- Hull Creek: brackish, shallow, mostly fresh, calm on most days. Sits 20 feet from the back porch. Source: `src/lib/site.ts`
- No rip currents or surf at Hull Creek; tidal creek, not open ocean.
- Cedar sauna, hot tub, dock, two kayaks, paddleboard, 2 screened porches. Source: `src/lib/site.ts` STANDOUT_AMENITIES
- Max 6 guests, 3 bedrooms, 1.5 baths. Source: `src/lib/site.ts`
- Reedville nearby (15 min): Victorian village, Reedville Market, Reedville Fishermen's Museum. Source: `src/lib/guidebook.ts`
- "No Bay Bridge" from the DC-getaways and dc-long-weekend posts: this is the Maryland Bay Bridge (MDTA) — the one DC Eastern Shore travelers face. Northern Neck does NOT require the Maryland Bay Bridge. OBX routes from DC also generally avoid the Maryland Bay Bridge (you go south, not east through Maryland) but the drive is ~5h regardless.
