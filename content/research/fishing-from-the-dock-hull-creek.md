### License and registration (primary sources)
- Code of Virginia § 28.2-302.5 exemptions include: a person under 16; "A person fishing from private real property that he owns or rents, the nonpaying guest of such person, or a member of the immediate family"; a person on a licensed charterboat or headboat. Quoted in `content/research/fall-rockfish-northern-neck.md` (https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title28.2/chapter3/section28.2-302.5/).
- VMRC Fisherman Identification Program (https://www.mrc.virginia.gov/fip/): "Registration ... is required for anglers age 16 and older who are exempt under state law from having to buy a saltwater fishing license." It is free ("a no-charge state program"). Anglers under 16 and paying charter or head boat customers do not have to register. The FAQ answers a dock question with: "Do I have to register too? Yes. If you don't have a license to fish in Virginia, you need to register with the FIP unless you're under the age of 16."
- **Unconfirmed:** whether a short-term-rental guest counts as someone who "rents" the property. The FIP page does not address rented property. Writer: say "Anglers 16 and older who don't hold a license register free with Virginia's Fisherman Identification Program, and under 16 needs nothing; check with VMRC about a rental stay." Do not claim guests are exempt.
