## Key facts

### Where the cottage sits, legally
- Hull Creek is a tidal creek fed by the Potomac; the property is in
  Northumberland County at about 37.95N, 76.45W, where the Potomac meets
  the Chesapeake Bay. Source: `src/lib/site.ts` (`PROPERTY`), the OBX post.
- Virginia manages its own tributaries of the Potomac under VMRC (Virginia
  Marine Resources Commission); the Potomac main stem is managed separately
  by the Potomac River Fisheries Commission (PRFC). Which VMRC section
  governs Hull Creek (Potomac-tributary vs Bay-tributary) is **not stated
  in the regulation text** (4VAC20-252-100 does not enumerate waters).
  Both sections carry the same slot and creel; only the opening date
  differs (see below). Writer: state the shared numbers, name both opening
  dates, and tell the reader to confirm the creek's classification with
  VMRC. Do not assert which one applies.

### Striped bass (rockfish), recreational, Virginia, current text
- **Chesapeake Bay and its tributaries, fall fishery** (4VAC20-252-90,
  last amended eff. 2024-04-01, Va. Register Vol. 40 Issue 18): open
  season "October 4 through December 31, inclusive"; minimum 19 inches
  total length, maximum 24 inches total length; "one fish per person" per
  day. Source: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title4/agency20/chapter252/section90/
- **Potomac River tributaries, summer/fall fishery** (4VAC20-252-100, last
  amended eff. 2024-04-01): open season "May 16 through July 6 and August
  21 through December 31"; same 19 to 24 inch slot; "one fish per person"
  per day. The section says its season "shall correspond to the open
  summer/fall season as established by the Potomac River Fisheries
  Commission for the mainstem Potomac River." Source:
  https://law.lis.virginia.gov/admincode/title4/agency20/chapter252/section100/
- **PRFC main stem Potomac** — UNVERIFIED. The PRFC "Blue Sheet" PDF
  (https://prfc.us/pdfs/BLUE-SHEET.pdf) fetched as unreadable binary. A
  third-party search summary claimed a main-stem closure "July 7 through
  October 17" reopening October 18, which conflicts with the VMRC text
  above ("August 21"). Do not state main-stem dates in the post. Point
  readers to prfc.us for the river proper.
- Two dates to keep straight for the reader: the Bay-side fall season
  opens **October 4**; the Potomac-tributary season is **already open**
  (since August 21) and both run **through December 31**. Slot and creel
  are identical: **19 to 24 inches, one fish per person per day.**
- Reedville charters fish the Bay and the Potomac mouth; the captain
  carries the rules. Post 3 (reedville-charter-fishing-guide) covers
  operators, prices, and booking; this post does not name operators or
  prices (none sourced here).

### Licence (Virginia saltwater recreational)
- Code of Virginia § 28.2-302.5 (amended eff. 2026-09-20, 2026 c. 834),
  exemptions include: "A person under the age of 16 or a person who has
  attained the age of 65"; "A person fishing from private real property
  that he owns or rents, the nonpaying guest of such person, or a member
  of the immediate family"; "A person fishing from a licensed headboat,
  charterboat, or pier licensed pursuant to § 28.2-302 or 28.2-302.8".
  Source: https://law.lis.virginia.gov/vacode/title28.2/chapter3/section28.2-302.5/
- **Interpretation, not sourced as such:** a cottage guest "rents" the
  property, so fishing from the private dock plausibly falls under the
  owner-or-renter exemption, and a charter passenger is covered by the
  charterboat exemption. Nobody at VMRC has confirmed the short-term-rental
  reading. Writer: say "you likely won't need your own license from the
  dock or on a charter, and it's a quick call to VMRC to be sure"; do not
  state it as settled. The no-cost Fisherman Identification Program (FIP)
  registration may still apply to license-exempt anglers; UNVERIFIED for
  this case.

### Season character (qualitative, keep to one paragraph)
- Fall is the big striped bass season on the lower Bay and lower Potomac:
  as the water cools, fish push out of the upper rivers and feed hard
  before winter; the largest fish show near the Bay mouth late in the
  season. This is general, widely reported angling knowledge, not a
  numeric claim; keep it to "as the water cools" and "late season," no
  temperatures, no dates beyond the regulatory ones above.
- Blue crab season overlaps in October (the crabbers post already covers
  the pot from the dock); a hot tub beside the porch and a sauna are the
  after-fishing amenities (`STANDOUT_AMENITIES`).
