### Full description (~150 words)
Captain's Cottage sits on Hull Creek in Heathsville, on the quiet end of
Virginia's Northern Neck — the brackish, shallow water where the Potomac meets
the Chesapeake Bay. It's an entire three-bedroom home that sleeps six, built
around the water: a private dock with crab pots, a cedar barrel sauna with a
west-facing window over the creek, a hot tub that runs year-round, and two
screened porches — one for dinner, one for doing nothing.

Days here are unhurried. Pull a crab pot at dawn, paddle one of the kayaks out
at slack tide, watch osprey and eagles work the creek, then warm up in the
sauna and plunge off the dock. The creek lights up gold every clear evening.
There's fast Wi-Fi and a quiet desk if you must work — but most guests don't.
Fifteen minutes to Reedville, under three hours from Washington, DC.
