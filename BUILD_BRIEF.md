## Website Build Prompt: Captain's Cottage Northern Neck STR

Build and optimize a short-term rental website for Captain's Cottage, a non-pet-friendly waterfront vacation property on Hull Creek in Heathsville, Virginia, in the Northern Neck region.

The site must be optimized for traditional SEO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) so it can rank in Google and Bing and be understandable to AI search tools like ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, and Google AI Overviews.

All bookings route to the existing Airbnb listing. Do not create Facebook, Instagram, a Facebook business page, on-site reviews, Google Business Profile, Bing Places, Apple Business Connect, or a direct-booking funnel at this stage.

Do not collect reviews on-site. Do not collect emails yet. Flag email capture and direct booking as future ideas only.

Position the site as a trusted Northern Neck travel guide first and a rental sales page second. Work through this checklist systematically. Confirm completion of each section before moving to the next.
